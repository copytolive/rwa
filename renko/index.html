Renko methodology & data sources
Traditional placement

Fixed absolute box. Continuation requires 1 box; reversal requires 2 boxes.

Smooth navigation

Mouse wheel zooms around the cursor, horizontal wheel or Shift+wheel pans, drag pans continuously, and pinch zoom works on touch devices. Older history loads without blocking the chart.

Historical source

Completed Binance 1-minute CLOSE bars are reconstructed in a Web Worker for responsive TradingView-style historical operation. The first view targets 50 Renko bricks and older pages are loaded progressively.

Live source

Realtime price and current Renko extension use Binance individual @trade ticks. The blue price line is the live venue price, not a synthetic execution price.

Crypto universe

All eligible Binance spot crypto pairs returned by the live exchange universe are searchable; the highest-liquidity 500 are pre-rendered for fast browsing.

Raw tick audit

Binance Vision individual trades archives remain available in the underlying audit engine. They are intentionally separate from the fast historical chart layer.
