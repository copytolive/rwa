Renko methodology, TradingView compatibility & raw-tick audit
Traditional placement

Fixed absolute box. Continuation requires 1 box; reversal requires 2 boxes. This follows TradingView's documented Traditional placement rule.

Fast historical source

Binance 1-minute CLOSE bars are used for the responsive history layer because TradingView historical Renko is also reconstructed from the underlying chart resolution rather than complete historical ticks.

Lazy history UX

The latest 50 bricks are the initial viewport. Moving backward fetches earlier 1-minute pages, rebuilds the loaded Renko window, and reveals older bricks progressively instead of downloading lifetime tick archives before first paint.

Raw-tick audit

The separate audit engine still uses Binance Vision spot trades archives containing individual executions. It is intentionally slower and is not silently substituted into the fast history layer.

All crypto universe

All eligible Binance spot crypto pairs are searchable. The highest-liquidity 500 are pre-rendered for speed; search is performed against the full loaded spot universe.

Execution

Renko brick levels are synthetic chart prices. Actual trading/fills must continue to use venue prices through the RWA execution path.
