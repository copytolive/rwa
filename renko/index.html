Traditional Renko methodology & raw-tick history integrity
Traditional placement

Fixed absolute box. Same Traditional placement rule used by TradingView: continuation needs one box; a reversal needs two boxes from the previous brick close.

Historical source

Binance Vision spot trades archives containing individual executions. This is intentionally more granular than TradingView historical Renko, which reconstructs history from its underlying chart resolution.

Live source

Binance Spot @trade individual-trade WebSocket. No kline, OHLC or aggregate-trade substitution.

Total history

Each selected coin automatically backfills from its oldest available raw-trade archive. ALL HISTORY shows the full historical overview; START shows the earliest bricks; LIVE returns to the current live chart.

500-market universe

The first 500 eligible crypto markets are shown by 24h USD-equivalent liquidity; the complete eligible universe remains searchable.

Execution

Renko prices are synthetic chart levels. Orders and fills must continue to use actual venue prices through the RWA execution path.
