Traditional Renko methodology & safety
Tick source

Bricks are calculated only from individual public trade events. No 1m/5m candle, OHLC or ATR source is used.

Traditional box

The box size is a fixed absolute price value. Same-direction continuation needs 1 box; a direction reversal needs 2 boxes.

Synthetic chart

Renko brick open/close values are synthetic. Trading and fills must still use the real venue price through the RWA execution path.
