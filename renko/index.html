TradingView observable parity contract and limits
Fixed 1-second source

Production is permanently fixed to 1-second source candles. No timeframe control exists. Deep history never silently switches to a higher resolution.

Total available history

Launch-pair origin timestamps are located from Binance historical fixed-1s rows and integrity-anchored to official Binance Public Data monthly checksums. Only bounded persistent origin windows are materialized; lifetime raw rows are never loaded into browser RAM at once.

GOLD long-history witness

XAU/USD uses Dukascopy public XAU-USD tick history aggregated to one-second bars, with coverage beginning 5 May 2003. It is explicit source ownership and is separate from XAUT/USDT listing age.

Realtime projection

Current 1-second bricks are provisional projections. They can change or disappear and become confirmed only when the 1-second source candle closes.

Traditional

Fixed absolute box. Continuation requires 1× box; reversal requires 2× box. No fake sub-tick bricks are created.

ATR

Wilder ATR is calculated from ordinary fixed 1-second OHLC candles and its positive raw value is used directly as the Renko box. XAUT/USDT deep history remains an on-demand OKX Spot fixed-1s path.

Wicks

Close source uses discarded Close extrema. OHLC source uses actual High/Low excursions.

Claim boundary

Observable parity is regression-tested. This page does not claim access to TradingView proprietary source code.
