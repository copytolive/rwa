RENKO V15 — tick-native formation rules
Formation

No visible or hidden chart timeframe controls formation. Historical formation consumes Binance aggregate trade events; live formation consumes individual Binance @trade events.

Immediate multi-brick

If one trade crosses five box thresholds, all five valid bricks lock immediately on that trade.

Traditional

Fixed absolute box. Same-direction continuation needs one box; reversal needs two boxes.

Percentage (LTP)

Percentage assigns one fixed box from the symbol-load LTP snapshot, including significant rounding and exchange minimum tick.

ATR

Tick ATR uses Wilder smoothing of absolute price changes between price-changing trade events. No candle or timeframe is used for ATR sizing or formation.

Wicks

Wicks record tick excursions that do not become body movement. Every confirmed body remains exactly one active box.

History

Older Binance aggregate trade events load in the background and when you pan left. No OHLC candle is used to form historical bricks.

Execution

Renko levels are synthetic thresholds. The blue REAL line and hard-stop check use current Binance market price.
