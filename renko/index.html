Traditional Renko methodology & raw-tick history integrity
Historical source

Binance Vision spot trades archives: individual executions. Missing archive segments keep status PARTIAL; they are never replaced by candles.

Live source

Binance Spot @trade individual-trade WebSocket. The last archived tick is joined to live venue ticks.

Traditional box

The box is a fixed absolute value. Continuation requires one box; reversal requires two boxes. AUTO/pct controls only select the fixed box size.

Total history

Every selected coin automatically starts lifetime raw-tick backfill in a Web Worker. Historical brick chunks are drawn progressively; after completion, START/ALL HISTORY/LIVE navigate the retained full Renko sequence.

500-market universe

The first 500 eligible crypto markets are shown by 24h USD-equivalent liquidity; the complete eligible universe remains searchable.

Synthetic chart

Renko brick prices are synthetic. Trading/fills must use actual venue prices through the RWA execution path.
