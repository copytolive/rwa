Traditional Renko methodology & safety
Tick source

Bricks are calculated only from individual public trade events. No candle, OHLC, kline, ATR or aggregate-trade source is used.

Traditional box

The box is a fixed absolute value. Continuation requires one box; reversal requires two boxes. AUTO and percentage buttons only choose a fixed box, then the engine remains Traditional.

Market universe

Crypto spot markets are ranked by 24h USD-equivalent liquidity. The first 500 are shown and the full eligible universe remains searchable.

Synthetic chart

Renko brick prices are synthetic. Trading and fills must use the real venue price through the RWA execution path.
