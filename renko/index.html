Renko methodology, TradingView compatibility & raw-tick audit
Traditional placement

Fixed absolute box. Continuation requires 1 box; reversal requires 2 boxes.

Fast historical source

Binance 1-minute CLOSE bars drive the responsive historical layer. This mirrors the historical-resolution approach used by TradingView-style Renko rather than pretending complete historical ticks are instant.

Trading terminal renderer

V7 adds a right price scale, bottom time scale, live venue-price line, crosshair, hover brick details and dense Renko geometry while keeping the underlying V6.1 history rules unchanged.

Lazy history UX

The latest 50 bricks are the initial viewport. Moving backward fetches earlier 1-minute pages and reveals older Renko progressively.

Raw-tick audit

The separate audit engine uses Binance Vision spot trades archives containing individual executions. It is intentionally separate from the fast historical layer.

All crypto universe

All eligible Binance spot crypto pairs returned by the live exchange universe are searchable. The highest-liquidity 500 are pre-rendered for speed.

Execution

Renko brick levels are synthetic chart prices. Actual fills continue to use venue prices through the RWA execution path.
